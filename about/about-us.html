About FixMyCar Hyderabad

We built FixMyCar because breakdowns don't wait for business hours — and neither do we.

Our story

FixMyCar started as an emergency roadside helpline for Hyderabad motorists. Today, our mobile mechanic network covers all of Hyderabad, the Outer Ring Road (ORR), and connecting national highways on a 24×7 standby, dispatched through one simple call or tap.

What we stand for

  • Verified mechanics — background-checked and skill-tested
  • Transparent pricing — quoted before work begins
  • Live tracking — know exactly when help arrives
  • 24×7 availability — highways, cities, no exceptions
