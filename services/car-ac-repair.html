Frequently asked questions

Why is my car AC not cooling?

The most common cause is low refrigerant due to a slow leak; a gas top-up with a leak test usually resolves it.

How often should AC gas be refilled?

Under normal use, once every 1-2 years is typical, sooner if there's an active leak.
